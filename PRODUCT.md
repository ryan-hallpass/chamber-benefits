# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are leaders and board members of the Ardmore Development Authority, Ardmore Tourism Authority, and Ardmore Chamber of Commerce. They evaluate marketing investments, approve scopes of work, and need visible evidence that the work advances the community's adopted Economic Vision Plan.

## Product Purpose

This repository contains client-facing digital proposals and working demonstrations for Hall Pass Digital's Ardmore engagements. The proposals help the three organizations understand, evaluate, and approve connected social media, tourism, member, employment, and destination-marketing programs.

## Positioning

Hall Pass combines national-caliber strategy, creative direction, video production, and digital systems with deep knowledge of Ardmore and local execution. The work is designed as one connected marketing engine rather than disconnected deliverables.

## Operating Context

Proposals are presented live to executives, executive committees, and boards, then shared as web links. Decisions involve public and nonprofit budgets, cross-organization funding, local procurement, measurable outcomes, and alignment with the 2025 Economic Vision Plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The three organizations are distinct but share audiences, priorities, and the Ardmore Means More brand.
- The signed ADA agreement runs October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026. Its Social Media Content & Management line item is $98,000 annually; travel shoots and long-form campaign videos are separate line items.
- The Chamber Member Engagement & Visibility Agreement remains active and must not be re-scoped or re-billed in these proposals.
- The broader next-year program is intended for executive-committee review, a November 2026 board retreat, and a January 1, 2027 start.
- A separate social-media bridge is needed for October through December 2026. It must use footage already captured by the final September shoot and include no new travel or on-location production.
- The bridge offers a $16,500 Focused Bridge and a recommended $24,500 Momentum Continuity option.
- The broader program should target a $225,000 annual price point and describe appropriate use of Ardmore-area vendors.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the established Ardmore Means More proposal voice: direct, evidence-led, optimistic, and plainspoken.
- Keep the established Hall Pass presentation identity and its sage, cream, ink, and warm-white palette.
- Keep slides concise enough to support a live presenter rather than replace one.

## Evidence on Hand

- Signed ADA Letter of Agreement and Exhibit A.
- September 9, 2026 meeting transcript with the requested bridge scope and November direction.
- Current proposal deck and working demonstrations in this repository.
- Verified performance evidence: 1.4 million cumulative video views, 100,000 engagements, about 11,000 followers gained, and 61% of reach outside Ardmore.

## Product Principles

- Show connected outcomes, not a collection of tactics.
- Make the board's decision and tradeoffs immediately understandable.
- Tie claims to signed scope, observed performance, or the Economic Vision Plan.
- Treat local procurement as a strength while preserving Hall Pass's specialized role.
- State exclusions and provisional pricing clearly.
